title: Activities link to your planned workout automatically
link: /calendar
linkLabel: Open the calendar
---
When a new ride arrives (upload or Strava / Intervals.icu sync) and you planned one workout for that day, it is now linked automatically and counts as completed.

- If several workouts are planned that day, the best match (discipline, then duration) is used when it is clear; otherwise you still get the suggestion.
- You see **Linked automatically** on the activity and in the planned workout, with **Change** and **Unlink**. After you unlink, that workout is never auto-linked again.
- Only new activities are linked; existing ones are not changed.
