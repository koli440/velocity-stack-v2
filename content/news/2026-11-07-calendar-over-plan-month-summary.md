The calendar got two upgrades.

- The week **time and TL bars** now show when you went **over plan**: a tick marks the plan and the extra is an amber striped segment with a `+N% over` label.
- A new **month summary** in the header shows time, distance, activities, TL and planned vs done for the displayed month.
