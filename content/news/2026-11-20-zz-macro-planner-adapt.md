title: The macro planner adapts to illness, races and life
---
For coaches using the macro planner: log an illness or injury and get a return-to-training ramp with replan options (shift, compress or lower the goal), each with a recomputed realism check and race-day form. Planned versus actual load now shows as a traffic light per week and proposes a review when it drifts. You can add B and C races, mark weeks with reduced availability, and optionally share your reasoning with the athlete on their read-only plan. Nothing changes until you apply an option, and applied changes are kept in an audit trail. Estimates only, not medical advice.
