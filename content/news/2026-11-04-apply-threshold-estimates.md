Threshold test runs now show a review card on the activity: each estimate next to your current FTP / LT1 / LT2, with the difference, assumptions and warnings.

- Edit any value, then apply it to your FTP or lactate threshold history, effective on the activity date. Nothing is applied automatically.
- If an entry already exists on that date you are asked before it is replaced.
