title: Zoom into the activity graph
---
Drag across the Activity graph to zoom into that part of the ride; click to return to the full view.

- All stacked lanes (speed, power, cadence, torque) zoom together, and the axis labels follow.
- Works with touch as well as mouse.
- Switching between Time and Distance resets the zoom.
