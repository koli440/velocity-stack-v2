You can now add VelocityStack to Claude as a custom connector. After you approve it, Claude can plan workouts in your calendar and review your training.

- **Plan:** create, edit, move, skip and delete planned workouts, with interval structures that are sent to Garmin, Wahoo or Intervals.icu like any other workout.
- **Review:** read your activities, run your analysis templates on them, and check fitness, fatigue and form.
- You choose the permissions on the consent page and can disconnect Claude any time on the Connections page.
