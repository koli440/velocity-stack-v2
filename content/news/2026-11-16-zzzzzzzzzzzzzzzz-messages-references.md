title: Reference an activity or workout in messages
link: /chat
linkLabel: Open messages
---
In a conversation, use the + button next to the message box to reference one of the athlete's recent activities or mention a planned workout. It only adds a link card to your message; it never links the activity to a workout or marks it completed.
