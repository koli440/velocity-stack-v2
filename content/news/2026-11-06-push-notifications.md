link: /settings
linkLabel: Turn on in Settings

When Velocity Stack is installed as an app, new notifications (followers, comments, mentions, coaching) can now pop up like any other system notification, even when the app is closed.

- Turn it on per device in **Settings**; your browser asks for permission only then.
- On iPhone/iPad, first add Velocity Stack to your Home Screen (iOS 16.4+).
- Use "Send test notification" to check it works.
