See how your training adds up. The new **Fitness** page and dashboard card show your Fitness (long-term load), Fatigue (short-term load) and Form (how fresh you are), with a chart and your calendar races marked.

- Rides without power get a load **estimated from heart rate** (needs your lactate threshold heart rate); these are shown as hatched bars and never change your rides.
- The first ~6 weeks show a "building baseline" note while the numbers settle.
