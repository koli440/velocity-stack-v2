title: Stats for the zoomed part of the Activity Graph
---
When you zoom into the Activity Graph, a stats strip now shows the numbers for exactly the visible range: duration, distance, average speed, heart rate, power, cadence and torque, plus **Normalized Power** (NP), max power/HR, and VI and IF when an FTP is known.

- It updates live as you zoom, pan or reset, and shows the whole activity when not zoomed.
- NP needs at least 30 seconds of selection and shows `-` for shorter ones.
- Time and distance views give the same numbers for the same stretch of the ride.
