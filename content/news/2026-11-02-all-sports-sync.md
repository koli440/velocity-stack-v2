Every source (Garmin, Strava, Wahoo, Intervals.icu and .fit uploads) now imports mountain bike, gravel, e-bike, indoor/virtual rides and runs, not only road rides. They count towards your fitness and fatigue and show with their own icons in the calendar and activity feed. Run load is estimated from heart rate.
