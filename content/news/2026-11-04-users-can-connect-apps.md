link: /connections
linkLabel: Open Connections

Apps that have no .fit export can now send your rides to Velocity Stack directly, after you allow it.

- Approve an app on a Velocity Stack consent screen; it can only upload rides (and read your FTP and weight if you let it).
- Uploaded rides get the same analysis, workload and fun names as rides from other sources, and are checked against duplicates.
- See your connected apps on the Connections page and disconnect any of them with one click.
- Developers can register an app at /developers.
