link: /settings
linkLabel: Open connections

With Wahoo connected, a planned ride can now be sent to your Wahoo calendar, including its intervals and power targets, so your ELEMNT can guide it. Send workouts one by one from the workout dialog, or turn on automatic sending on the Wahoo card. If you connected Wahoo before, reconnect it once to grant the new permission.
