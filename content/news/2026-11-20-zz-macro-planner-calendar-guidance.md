title: Macro plan guidance in the athlete's calendar
---
Coaches using the macro planner now see the plan right in the athlete's calendar: a band per week with the phase, focus, target TL, TL done and still to schedule, race markers (A/B/C) and a Hard/Easy/Rest hint per day, so workouts can be planned against it. Athletes see it in their own calendar only if their coach chooses to share it.
