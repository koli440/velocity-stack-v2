title: Coaches get a long-term macro planner
---
Coaches are getting a long-term planner for the athletes they coach: one A race, weekly training-load targets in mesocycles (3+1, 2+1, 4+1 or custom), a projection of fitness, fatigue and form up to race day, a realism check against what the athlete has actually sustained, and a taper estimate. When your coach publishes a plan you see it as a read-only timeline under Training, Training plan. No workouts are planned, only the weekly shape.
