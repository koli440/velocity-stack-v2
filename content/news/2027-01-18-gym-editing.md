title: Log and edit gym sessions
---
You can now create a gym session by hand ("+ Log gym session" in your feed) and edit imported ones: add, remove, reorder and rename exercises, and set reps, weight (kg or lb), RPE (with optional RIR), tempo, set type (warm-up, working, drop, failure), rest, side, bodyweight or assisted load and notes. Optional bar-velocity fields are available too.

- Volume, best set and an estimated 1RM (Epley, an estimate) are calculated for you.
- Edited sessions are never overwritten by re-imports.
- Your coach can edit your sessions; their changes are labelled and recorded in your audit trail.
