title: Respect now sits next to the date, with levels
---
**Respect** is now a glass tile right next to the activity date, on the activity page and in the feed. It shows the number of respects with the word "Respect", plus a level word that climbs as one activity collects more: Nod, Props, Solid, Strong, Beast, Machine, Legend, Mythic, Immortal and Godlike.
