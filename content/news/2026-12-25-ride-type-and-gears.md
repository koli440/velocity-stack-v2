link: /settings
linkLabel: Open settings

Activities now have a ride type (road or track; set a default in Settings). Track rides can carry several gears with notes, and you can assign a gear to time segments with a slider over the power, cadence and speed chart. Fixed-gear speed and PB Vault prefill follow the gear in use, and your coach can see and edit ride type and gears. Existing rides are unchanged and count as road.
