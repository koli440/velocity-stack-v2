title: Tablets use the mobile layout, menu fixed
---
On tablets and narrow windows (up to 1023px) VelocityStack now uses the compact mobile layout: hamburger menu, slim top bar and the calendar as a stacked day list, so nothing is squeezed or overflows on an iPad in portrait.

The navigation menu also opens properly again when you tap the hamburger: it slides in above the dark overlay with all items visible, and closes when you tap the overlay, press Esc or pick a page.
