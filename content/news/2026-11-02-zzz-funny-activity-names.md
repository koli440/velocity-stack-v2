New imports from Strava, Wahoo, Intervals.icu, Garmin and file uploads no longer arrive as "Morning Ride" or "Garmin Sync". If the source only gave a generic name, we now pick a funny one that fits the session and the time of day, like "Sunrise Sufferfest: Hill Grudge" or "Lunch Break Heist: Rack Attack". Rides, MTB, gravel, e-bike, runs and gym sessions each have their own big pool, and we avoid repeating your recent names. Names you or your source chose are never replaced, and once you rename an activity it stays yours. Existing activities are not renamed.
