title: Load estimates for LT, zone and watt workouts
link: /calendar
linkLabel: Open the calendar
---
Planned workouts with %LT1 / %LT2 targets, power zones (Z1-Z6) or absolute watts now show a "~TL" load estimate on the calendar and in the workout dialog, and count in the fitness forecast instead of being listed as "no estimate".

- Uses the rider's FTP, lactate thresholds and zones for that day. Without lactate thresholds it assumes LT1 = 75% and LT2 = 100% of FTP (marked with `*`).
- Heart-rate, cadence and speed targets still give no estimate.
