title: Gym sessions now have a training load
link: /fitness
linkLabel: Open Fitness
---
Gym sessions with recorded sets now get an **estimated training load**, based on how hard and how explosive your exercises were (no effort rating needed). Open a gym session and tap **How this was estimated** to see the details.

The load shows on the calendar and counts in **Fitness, fatigue & form** as an estimate. Prefer to keep fitness to rides and runs? Turn off **Include gym sessions in fitness** in Settings. Your coach sees your choice and the same breakdown.
