title: Give a ride some respect
---
You can now tap **Respect** on any activity you can see, a quick +1 without writing a comment. Tap again to take it back.

- The count shows on the activity and in the feed; owners also see who gave respect.
- You get one notification per activity ("ann and 3 others gave respect"), not one per tap.
- In **Settings** you can turn respect notifications off, or stop accepting respect on your activities.
