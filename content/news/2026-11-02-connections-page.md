Your ride sources now live on a stand-alone **Connections** page, linked from Settings, in order of preference: **Garmin** (coming soon), **Wahoo**, **SRM**, **Intervals.icu** and **Strava**.

- SRM (PC8/PC7/PC6/PC5) has no cloud sync: import its `.fit` files.
- Intervals.icu stays the bridge for Zwift, MyWhoosh and others; Strava is a secondary source.
