title: Push planned workouts to Intervals.icu
link: /settings
linkLabel: Connection settings
---
Planned workouts can be sent to your **Intervals.icu** calendar, so they show up on your head unit.

- Push or update a single workout from the planned workout dialog, or switch on automatic push in your connection settings.
- A coach who plans your workouts can push them to your calendar too, without ever seeing your API key.
