The main menu is now grouped into Training, Lab, Tools, Community and Settings sections that open and close, and it remembers which ones you keep open.

- **Messages** lists your most recent conversations with unread counts.
- **Rider Telemetry** has direct links to *My sessions* and *Following*.
- On phones and tablets a bottom bar gives one-tap access to Home, Calendar, Messages and the full menu.
