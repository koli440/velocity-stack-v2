Pool and open-water swims from Strava, Garmin, Wahoo, Intervals.icu and .fit/.tcx/.gpx uploads now appear in your calendar and activity feed with their own swim icon. Distance is shown in metres with a pace per 100 m. Swims with heart rate get a regular heart-rate based training load; swims without heart rate count with a conservative default intensity, so they still add to your fitness, fatigue and form.
