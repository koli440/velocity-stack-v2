When you view an athlete, their pages (Overview, Activities, Calendar, Fitness, Personal Bests, Templates, Physiology and Lab with its tests) are now in the menu under "Viewing" plus the athlete's name instead of tabs on the page. On phones the bottom bar switches to Overview, Activities, Calendar and Menu. Switches such as the Fitness range and Velodromes view share one orange tab style with larger touch targets.
