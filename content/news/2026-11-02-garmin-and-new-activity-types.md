**Garmin is here.** Connect your Garmin account on the **Connections** page, where your sources now appear in order of preference: Garmin, Wahoo, SRM, Intervals.icu and Strava.

- New rides and gym sessions you record after connecting are imported automatically. Garmin only sends new activities, so use Intervals.icu or a `.fit` upload for your history.
- Send planned workouts to Garmin Connect, by hand from the workout dialog or automatically.

**More activity types, from every source.** Mountain bike, gravel, e-bike, indoor and virtual rides, and runs (including trail and treadmill) now sync from Garmin, Strava, Wahoo, Intervals.icu and `.fit` uploads.

- They count towards your fitness, fatigue and form. Run load is estimated from heart rate.
- Each type has its own icon in the calendar and activity feed.
