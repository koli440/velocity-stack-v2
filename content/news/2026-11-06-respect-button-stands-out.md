title: Respect is easier to spot
---
On an activity page, **Respect** is now a bold orange button right below the title, full-width on mobile. After you tap it, it turns into **Respected** with a check. Read-only viewers see a clearer "3 respects" badge. In the feed, the Respect button is also bigger and easier to tap, with the label shown on wider screens.
