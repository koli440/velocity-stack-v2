New help page: plug your SRM PowerControl 8 into your computer, copy the `.fit` files from `SRM/Activities`, and drop them into **Import History**. It also covers older PC5/6/7 units and `.fit` files from other devices. The guide is linked from the import dialog and the Help menu.
