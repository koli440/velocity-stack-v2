title: Gym sessions are no longer shown as rides
---
Gym (strength) sessions now show as "Gym" on your calendar, in the feed and in search, instead of "Ride". They are also left out of ride counts, such as your public ride count and your coach's weekly ride totals, and can't be linked to race entries or ride workouts.
