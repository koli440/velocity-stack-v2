Coaches now see each athlete's **Fitness, Fatigue and Form** on the coach dashboard, with the weekly ramp and flags for very negative form, a ramp that is too fast, or a baseline still being built. The dashboard can be sorted by any of them.

- Each athlete has a **Fitness** tab with the full chart, range selector and calendar races.
- Values use the athlete's own FTP and heart rate thresholds, and only while the coaching relationship is active.
