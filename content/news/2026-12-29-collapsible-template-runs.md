title: Collapsible template runs on activities
---
The Template Runs card on an activity can now be collapsed, and so can each run inside it.

- With several runs, only the most recent starts open; the others show a one-line summary (result and verdict).
- Whether the whole card is collapsed is remembered in this browser.
