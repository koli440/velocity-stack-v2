title: Route maps can be shared with other riders
link: /settings
linkLabel: Choose who sees your maps
---
Riders who can see your activity can now see its **route map** too. In **Settings → Privacy zone** choose who gets it: only you, your coach, your coach and followers (default) or everyone who can see the activity.

Your privacy zone is always respected: GPS points inside it are removed on our servers before anything is sent, so they can never leak.
