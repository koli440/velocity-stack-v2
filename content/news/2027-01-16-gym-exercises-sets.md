title: Gym sessions now show exercises and sets
---
Gym sessions imported from a .fit file (upload, bulk, Intervals.icu or Wahoo) now keep their exercises, sets, reps, weights and rest, shown on the activity page next to your heart rate. Sources without set data, like Strava, still give duration, heart rate and calories. Earlier gym sessions from your connected accounts are imported again over the next syncs, without touching your rides.
