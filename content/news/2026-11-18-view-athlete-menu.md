The menu you see while viewing an athlete is now grouped like the main menu: Training, Lab (with Tests, Analysis and Data sections), Personal Bests, Physiology and Notes.

- **Messages** now opens the chat with that athlete and keeps you in their view, with their unread badge.
- The mobile bottom bar shows Overview, Activities, Calendar, Messages and Menu.
