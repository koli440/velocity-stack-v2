title: Browse activity photos without closing them
---
When an activity has several photos, the enlarged view now has previous and next buttons and a position counter such as "2 / 5".

- Use the arrow keys on a keyboard (Esc closes), or swipe left and right on a touch screen.
- Navigation wraps around from the last photo to the first.
