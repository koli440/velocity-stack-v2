title: Coaches can apply threshold estimates to a rider
link: /dsl-guide
linkLabel: Template syntax guide

An active coach can now review a rider's threshold test estimates on the rider's activity and apply FTP / LT1 / LT2 to the rider's history.

- Applied entries show "From template estimate" with a link to the activity, and who set them; the rider is notified as for other coach edits.
- Only the rider and their active coach can write; nothing is applied automatically.
