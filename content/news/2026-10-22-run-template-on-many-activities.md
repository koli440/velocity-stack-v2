title: Run a template on many activities at once
link: /templates
linkLabel: Open templates
---
On the Templates page, use **Run on activities…** to analyse several rides with one template. Search activities by name, add up to 50 to the list and run them in one go, with progress and a summary of what worked, what had no matching efforts and what was skipped.

- Activities that already have a run of that template are re-run (the result is replaced, no duplicates).
- Coaches can do the same on an athlete's Templates page; those runs are marked as run by coach.
