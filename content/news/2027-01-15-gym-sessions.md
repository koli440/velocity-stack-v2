title: Gym sessions are now imported
---
Strength and gym sessions now come in from Intervals.icu, Strava, Wahoo and .fit uploads (single or bulk), with duration, heart rate and calories. They show in your feed and calendar and can complete planned gym workouts, but never count toward ride stats, fitness or training load. Exercises and sets are coming next.
