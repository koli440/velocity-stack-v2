title: Planned time in the week summary, and links that stick
link: /calendar
linkLabel: Open the calendar
---
The calendar's week summary now shows **planned time** next to the time you actually rode, with a progress bar. A `+` means some planned workouts have no computable duration.

- Skipped workouts are left out; missed and completed ones count as planned.
- A planned workout linked to a ride is no longer shown as *Missed* when the ride is later replaced by a better copy (e.g. from Intervals.icu). Linked workouts always show as completed, and you can still **Unlink** an activity in the workout dialog.
