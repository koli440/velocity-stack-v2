title: Enlarge the activity graph
---
The activity graph on the activity page now has an Enlarge button that opens it in a large pop-over (full screen on phones). In the enlarged view you can drag to zoom into a range, zoom in/out and pan with buttons or the keyboard (arrow keys, + / −, 0 to reset), and use Reset zoom. The zoom stays in sync with the graph on the page.
