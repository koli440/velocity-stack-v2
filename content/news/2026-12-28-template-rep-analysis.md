Template runs now show how every rep compares with a reference you choose: your previous run, your personal best, the median of your last 5 runs, or any past run.

- Rep table with a dashed ghost line, fade curve, first-half vs second-half, worst rep, slope and consistency.
- Flags for reps well below your median and for missing reps ("7 of 8 reps detected").
- Heart-rate recovery between reps and a trend chart over all runs.
- Coaches see the same views in athlete context. Older runs are refreshed in the background.
