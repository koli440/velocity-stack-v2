You can now connect your Garmin account under Settings → Connections. New rides and gym sessions you record after connecting are imported automatically, and planned ride workouts can be sent to your Garmin calendar, by hand or automatically. Garmin only sends new activities, so use Intervals.icu or a .fit upload for your history.
