Any account can now coach, or be coached.

- Invite a rider as your athlete, or ask someone to be your coach. A rider has one active coach, and accepting is the consent.
- Coaches see the athlete's activities, templates and Personal Bests, and can edit their FTP, weight and training settings. Edits are labelled **set by coach** and the rider is notified.
- Open **Coach dashboard** from the user menu to see your athletes. The rider can end coaching at any time.
