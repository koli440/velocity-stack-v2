title: Gear Architect shows your records and races
link: /gears
linkLabel: Open Gear Architect
---
Pick a gear in **Gear Architect** and see your **PB Vault records** and **race rides** that used it, including gears used in ride segments, with date, event or venue, power and time. Activities now have a **Race** tag you (or your coach) can set; linking an activity to a calendar race tags it automatically. Unlinking only removes a tag that was added automatically.
