A new page explains what Claude can do with your VelocityStack account, which permissions it asks for and how to disconnect it.

- **Claude Code** can now connect too: `claude mcp add --transport http velocity-stack https://www.velocity-stack.app/api/mcp`, then sign in with `/mcp`.
- Claude now asks before every change it makes to your calendar.
