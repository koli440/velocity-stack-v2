title: Workouts are sent with hard watts from your zones
link: /connections
linkLabel: Open Connections
---
Workouts sent to Garmin, Wahoo and Intervals.icu now carry absolute watts calculated from your Velocity Stack FTP, LT1/LT2 and your own power zones, instead of percentages that depend on the setup of the other platform or device. The "custom zones are not applied" limitation is gone.

Workouts that were already sent are updated automatically. Without an FTP or LT power in your profile you get a clear note and the step keeps its length.
