title: Garmin workouts no longer duplicate after reconnecting
link: /connections
linkLabel: Open Connections
---
Reconnecting Garmin used to make Velocity Stack forget which workouts it had already sent, so they showed as "Not in Garmin" and were sent again next to the old ones. Reconnecting the same Garmin account now keeps that link.

The Garmin settings also show an error instead of disappearing if they cannot be loaded.
