Plan training on the **Calendar**.

- Create a workout from one of your templates, or write the structure yourself in the template syntax. A live chart previews it.
- A planned workout is linked to the ride you did on the same day, and shows as completed, skipped or missed.
- Coaches can plan workouts for their athletes.
