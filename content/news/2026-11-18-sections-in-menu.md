Lab tools, Admin pages, Riders (Find / Following) and Templates (Public / Mine / Favorites) now live in the menu instead of tab strips on each page, so they look and behave the same everywhere.

- A small breadcrumb at the top of these pages, like *Lab › Durability*, shows where you are.
- Existing links such as `/riders?tab=following` keep working.
