link: /fitness

The Fitness page (and the coach's athlete Fitness tab) can now project fitness, fatigue and form 2 to 8 weeks ahead from your planned workouts, shown as dashed lines.

- Races on the chart show the projected form on race day, and calendar race chips show it too.
- Unplanned days, skipped workouts and workouts without a load estimate count as zero training; a note tells you when the forecast is partial.
