Gym sessions now use a shared **exercise list** instead of free typing. Search by name or by other names (try "legpress"), pick several exercises at once, and your most recent ones come first. No more typos, and lunges and other one-side-at-a-time exercises are counted correctly.

Box jumps and other jumps are logged in **centimetres**, with optional added weight. Missing an exercise? Suggest it: you can use it right away, and once the admin approves it everyone gets it. Your load estimates use the list for better accuracy and older sessions are matched automatically.
