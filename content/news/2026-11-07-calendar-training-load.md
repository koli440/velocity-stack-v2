The calendar now shows **training load (TL)** much clearer.

- Completed activities show their real TL (`TL 85`, estimates as `TL ~85`); planned workouts show a dashed `~TL 80 planned`.
- Day totals of finished days use only what you actually did; today shows done + remaining plan.
- The week summary has a new **TL bar** (done vs planned), next to the time bar.
