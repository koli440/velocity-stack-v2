link: /connections

Workouts sent to your Garmin (Edge, watch) now show **lap (interval) average power** against the target, the same way Intervals.icu workouts do.

- Prefer current power, or a 3, 10 or 30 second average? Pick it on the Garmin card in **Connections**.
- Changing it re-sends your planned workouts automatically, so the device always matches your choice.
