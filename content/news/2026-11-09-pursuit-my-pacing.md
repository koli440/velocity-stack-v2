When signed in, Pursuit Strategist can now shape the lap-by-lap plan like your typical pacing over the same distance, learned from the lap splits of your PB Vault records. Your first lap is the average of your real first laps, with newer records counting more. Your target time is kept, the generic plan is shown alongside, and you see how many records were used and, lap by lap, whether you are speeding up or slowing down compared with the previous lap. Without records for that distance, the generic model is used and the reason is shown.
