title: Macro planner races now come from the calendar
---
For coaches using the macro planner: the A, B and C races are now picked from the athlete's calendar instead of typed in. If the athlete has no suitable race, add it to their calendar first and select it in the planner. Renaming or moving the race in the calendar updates the plan, and a moved or deleted race flags the plan so nothing is lost silently.
