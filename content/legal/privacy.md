## 2. Data we collect
**Account data**
- Your email address and a password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider, never in readable form).
- Your name or nickname. If you sign in with Google we receive your Google name and email address, and nothing else is used (see section 8). We do not copy your Google profile photo.
- Optional profile details you add: a profile photo you upload yourself, home velodrome, default gearing, crank length, ride type and display preferences.

**Training data**
- Activity files you upload (FIT files) and rides imported from Strava or Intervals.icu, with the metrics we derive from them: power, speed, cadence, heart rate and other sensor streams, distance, time, curves, training load and similar telemetry.
- **Location data:** activities may contain GPS coordinates and routes. They are shown on maps in the app. You can set a privacy zone around your home so that other riders see the route masked inside that zone.
- **Health-related data:** heart rate and other physiological measurements, weight, FTP and other threshold values (for example lactate thresholds) and the power zones derived from them.
- Gear, templates and run results, personal bests, aero tests, planned workouts, calendar notes and races, comments, photos you attach to rides, and the feedback or bug reports you send us.

**Relationship and activity data**
- Who you follow and who follows you, blocks, coaching invitations and relationships, and in-app notifications about them.

**Technical data**
- Your session (see section 11), basic server logs kept by our hosting and database providers (such as IP address, browser type and time of request), and a record of the edits a coach makes to your settings.

We do not collect payment data. The "Buy me a coffee" button is a link to an external service.

## 3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis
- **To provide the Service (contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)):** creating and securing your account, importing and analysing your rides, showing your data to you, running templates, planning, notifications.
- **Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** keeping the Service secure, preventing abuse, fixing bugs, rate-limiting integrations, and basic service statistics. We balance these interests against your rights and do not use your data for advertising.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)):** heart rate, weight, thresholds and similar measurements can reveal information about your health, which is a special category of data. We process it only to provide the features you use, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by uploading or connecting that data and by using these features. Sharing your data with a coach is likewise based on your explicit consent: it only happens after you accept a coaching relationship. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 13); withdrawing does not affect processing done before.
- **Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)):** where the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

## 4. Who can see your data
- **You** see all of your own data.
- **Other riders:** only the activities and profile details that the Service shows to other riders (for example to followers), with privacy-zone areas masked. A rider can require approval for new followers. Your raw GPS files, connection keys and email address are never shown to other riders.
- **Comments and public profile:** your name, profile photo and the comments you write are visible to the riders who can see the related ride or profile.
- **Administrators** of the Service can access data where needed to operate the Service, handle your requests, moderate content and fix problems.

## 5. Coaching
Coaching works only with your consent.
- A coaching relationship starts only after the rider accepts. Either side can send the invitation; accepting it (or requesting a coach yourself) is your consent. A rider has at most one active coach at a time.
- **Only after you approve**, your coach can see your training data, including private activities, curves, templates, personal bests, calendar and settings. Privacy-zone areas stay masked on maps, and your email address, connection keys and raw GPS files are never visible to your coach.
- Your coach can change only your training settings (FTP, weight, thresholds, power zones, training defaults) and your planned workouts, races and calendar notes. Every such edit is labelled "set by coach", recorded in an audit list, and you are notified.
- You can end coaching or block a coach at any time and access stops immediately. A coach can also leave. Data that a coach created for you (for example planned workouts) stays in your account.
- If you are a coach, you are responsible for handling the athlete's data you can see confidentially and only for coaching that athlete.

## 6. Integrations (Strava and Intervals.icu)
Integrations start only when you connect them under Settings, Connections.
- **Strava:** you authorise us through Strava's own login (permission to read your activities). We import your rides and activity data (streams such as power, heart rate and GPS, and basic ride details) and store the access tokens on our servers. Strava notifies us of new rides so we can import them.
- **Intervals.icu:** you give us your athlete ID and API key. We import your rides and, if you use the feature, push planned workouts to your Intervals.icu calendar. The key is stored write-only and is not readable from your browser. It is never visible to other riders or coaches.
- **Disconnecting:** you can disconnect either integration in Settings at any time. This deletes the stored tokens or key, stops syncing and, for Strava, revokes our access at Strava. You can also revoke access in your Strava or Intervals.icu account. Rides that were already imported stay in your account until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
- Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We receive data from them only as you authorise.

## 7. Service providers and third parties
We use the following providers to run the Service. They process data on our behalf, except where noted.
- **Supabase** – database, authentication and file storage (profile photos).
- **Vercel** – hosting of the website and its server functions.
- **Google** – "Continue with Google" sign-in, only if you choose it. Google acts as an independent controller for your Google account.
- **Strava and Intervals.icu** – only if you connect them, as described in section 6.
- **Map tile providers (MapTiler, or OpenStreetMap)** – when you view a map, your browser requests map images from the provider, which therefore receives your IP address and the map area you view. Your activity data is not sent to them.
- **Email delivery provider (Resend)** – if email notifications are switched on, we send notification emails (for example about coaching or follow requests) to your account email address through this provider. You can turn this off in your settings.
- **Google Analytics** – only if the operator has enabled it on the deployment; the Service works without it. If enabled, it measures page usage and may use cookies.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

VelocityStack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. In particular we do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security purposes or where the law requires.

## 9. International transfers
Our providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States). Where that happens we rely on the providers' appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

## 10. How long we keep data and how to delete it
- We keep your data for as long as your account exists.
- **Disconnecting an integration** deletes its tokens or key immediately. **Individual items** such as rides, templates, notes, races and your profile photo can be deleted by you in the app.
- **Deleting your account:** the app does not currently have a self-service "delete account" button. To delete your account and the associated data (activities, files, settings and relationships), email **koli440@gmail.com** from your account address. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm to you. Backups held by our providers are overwritten in their normal cycle.
- Server logs at our providers are kept for a short period set by those providers.
- We may keep limited data longer only where the law requires or to establish or defend legal claims.

## 11. Cookies and local storage
We use only what is strictly necessary and do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
- Your **sign-in session** is stored in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. It is removed when you sign out.
- **Preferences** such as theme and display options are stored in your browser.
- If the operator enables Google Analytics (section 7), it may set analytics cookies; otherwise none are set by us.

Because these items are essential to provide the service you request, no consent banner is needed for them.

## 12. Security
Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is controlled by row-level security in the database so that each account reads its own rows, and coaches and riders see only what the rules in this policy allow. Passwords are hashed by the authentication provider, integration secrets are stored server-side and are not readable from the browser, and privileged operations run on the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 13. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you have the right to:
- access your data and receive a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected (most of it you can edit in the app);
- have your data erased;
- receive your data in a portable format (you can also download your own original files from the app where offered, and we will provide an export on request);
- restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent at any time, for example by ending coaching, disconnecting an integration, or asking us to delete your account;
- complain to your local data protection supervisory authority.

To use these rights, email **koli440@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within one month.
