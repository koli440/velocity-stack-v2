Strava is great at the social side of riding. If what you are missing is the analysis (what your power actually did over the session, rep by rep), you might be looking for something to sit next to it or replace it. Here is what VelocityStack offers, honestly.

## What VelocityStack does differently

- **Interval templates:** describe a session once, detect every rep in a ride, and compare it to your previous run, your best or a median of your last five.
- **Track focus:** gear tracking, velodromes, race tagging and a PB Vault. No GPS on the velodrome? Speed and distance are worked out from cadence and gear.
- **Fitness, Fatigue and Form** and a power profile from your rides.
- **Coaching** with rider consent.

## What it is not

It is not a social network built around segments and kudos. Many riders keep Strava for that and use VelocityStack for the numbers, which is why connecting Strava is supported.

## Moving your rides across

1. [Export your Strava data](/blog/export-strava-data) and import the ZIP, or [connect Strava](/import/strava).
2. Connect [Garmin](/import/garmin), Wahoo or Intervals.icu so new rides arrive directly.
3. Or import [.fit files](/import/fit) from any bike computer.

Your rides stay in Strava; nothing is deleted there. Pick your source on the [import page](/import) and you can see your rides and fitness in a few minutes.
