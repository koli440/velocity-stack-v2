If you use Velocity Stack from the infield, between efforts, or on the way home from training, you don't want to type the address or search for a bookmark every time. You can put it on your home screen, and it opens like any other app.

There's no app store involved. Velocity Stack is a web app, and your phone can install it straight from the browser.

## iPhone and iPad

Use Safari. Other iPhone browsers handle this differently.

1. Open [velocity-stack.app](/) and log in.
2. Tap the **Share** button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

![Safari on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen](/blog/install-iphone.webp)

The icon appears on your home screen. Open Velocity Stack from there and it runs full screen, without the browser bars.

## Android

Use Chrome.

1. Open [velocity-stack.app](/) and log in.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top right.
3. Tap **Install app**, or **Add to Home screen** if you don't see that.
4. Confirm.

![Chrome on Android: open the menu, then tap Install app](/blog/install-android.webp)

Other Android browsers have a similar entry in their menu.

## Why bother

- **One tap to open.** No address to type, no tab to find.
- **Full screen.** It looks and feels like an app.
- **Notifications on iPhone.** Pop-up notifications only work when the app has been added to the home screen and opened from there. This needs iOS 16.4 or newer.

## If you can't see the option

- On iPhone, check that you're in Safari and not inside another app's built-in browser.
- On Android, update Chrome and reload the page.
- On either, open the page in the full browser and not from a link inside another app.
